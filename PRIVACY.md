# Privacy Policy — Instagram Following Feed Quickview

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any third party.

- **Cookies:** Instagram session cookies are read solely to authenticate requests to Instagram's own API on your behalf. They are used in a browser-session request rule, never written to extension storage, and never sent anywhere other than Instagram's servers.
- **Feed data:** Fetched posts are cached locally in your browser using Chrome's local storage API. This data never leaves your device.
- **Settings:** User preferences (time window, App ID) are stored locally in your browser and never transmitted.

All network requests made by this extension go exclusively to `https://www.instagram.com`.
